Help

How to play
  • Garden: tap an empty plot to plant a seed you own. Plants grow in real time. Tap a ripe plant to harvest coins and XP. Ripe crops slowly wilt (down to half value) if you leave them too long.
  • Tools you own add garden powers (watering, boosts) with a recharge.
  • Packs: a free Seed Packet every 6 hours, daily login rewards, level-ups and battles. Duplicates level cards up (to Lv 5): faster growth, bigger harvests, stronger in battle.
  • Battle (optional): pick a stage, spend sunlight to place plants and use tools/weather from your deck of 8. Don't let 3 pests through the fence.
  • Decor goes in decor slots for passive bonuses. You get more slots every 5 levels.
